POLICY ID: POL-21
TITLE: Third-Party Data Sharing Policy
CATEGORY: Third-Party Sharing
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2024-01-01
OWNER: Data Protection Office

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls when customer or confidential data may be shared with third parties and subcontractors.

2. REQUIREMENTS
2.1 Customer or confidential data must not be shared with any third party or subcontractor without prior written authorization from the company.
2.2 Customer data may only be shared with approved third parties that are bound by written data protection and confidentiality obligations no less protective than those in the contract with the company.
2.3 A vendor remains fully responsible for the acts and omissions of any subcontractor to which it discloses company data.
2.4 Any unauthorized disclosure of company data must be reported to the company within twenty-four (24) hours of discovery.

3. APPLICABILITY
Applies to all vendors, contractors, and partners that receive customer or confidential data from the company.
