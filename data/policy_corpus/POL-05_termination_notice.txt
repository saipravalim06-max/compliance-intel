POLICY ID: POL-05
TITLE: Contract Termination and Notice Policy
CATEGORY: Termination
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2024-01-01
OWNER: Legal Department

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines minimum notice periods required for termination of company contracts across categories.

2. REQUIREMENTS
2.1 Termination for convenience (without cause) requires a minimum notice period of thirty (30) calendar days, unless a longer period is specified by a category-specific policy (e.g., critical vendors require 60 days per POL-01).
2.2 Termination for cause requires a cure period of no less than fifteen (15) calendar days following written notice of the breach, except in cases of material data security breaches, which may permit immediate termination.
2.3 All termination notices must be delivered in writing to a designated notice address specified in the contract.
2.4 Contracts must not permit unilateral termination by a counterparty with less than fifteen (15) days notice under any circumstance.

3. APPLICABILITY
Applies to all commercial contracts entered into by the company, unless superseded by a more specific policy for a given contract category.
