POLICY ID: POL-06
TITLE: Limitation of Liability Policy
CATEGORY: Liability
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2024-01-01
OWNER: Legal Department

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes acceptable standards for liability caps and exclusions in company contracts.

2. REQUIREMENTS
2.1 A counterparty's total liability under a contract should not be capped below an amount equal to twelve (12) months of fees paid or payable under the agreement, except where a lower cap is separately approved by the General Counsel.
2.2 Exclusions of liability for gross negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of confidentiality obligations are not acceptable and must be carved out of any liability cap or exclusion clause.
2.3 Uncapped liability is required for breaches of data protection obligations resulting in a reportable data breach.
2.4 Any mutual liability cap must apply equally to both parties unless a business justification for asymmetry is documented and approved.

3. APPLICABILITY
Applies to all commercial contracts where the company is a counterparty, particularly vendor, software licensing, and services agreements.
