POLICY ID: POL-04
TITLE: Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Policy
CATEGORY: Confidentiality
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2024-01-01
OWNER: Legal Department

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes minimum confidentiality protection standards for agreements involving the exchange of confidential or proprietary information.

2. REQUIREMENTS
2.1 Confidentiality obligations in vendor and partner contracts must survive termination of the underlying agreement for a minimum period of three (3) years.
2.2 Confidentiality obligations relating to trade secrets must survive indefinitely, or for as long as the information retains trade secret status.
2.3 Any exceptions to confidentiality obligations (e.g., disclosures required by law) must require prior written notice to the company where legally permissible.
2.4 Confidential information must be marked or otherwise identified as confidential at the time of disclosure, or within a reasonable period thereafter.
2.5 Return or destruction of confidential materials must be required within thirty (30) days of contract termination.

3. APPLICABILITY
Applies to all agreements involving the disclosure of company confidential, proprietary, or trade secret information to a third party.
