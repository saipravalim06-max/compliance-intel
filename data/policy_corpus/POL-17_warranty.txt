POLICY ID: POL-17
TITLE: Warranty Policy
CATEGORY: Warranty
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2024-01-01
OWNER: Legal Department

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines minimum warranty standards required in vendor and supplier contracts.

2. REQUIREMENTS
2.1 Software and service vendors must provide a minimum warranty period of ninety (90) days from delivery or go-live, during which defects must be remedied at no additional cost.
2.2 Vendors must warrant that delivered products/services do not infringe upon the intellectual property rights of any third party.
2.3 Disclaimers of implied warranties (e.g., merchantability, fitness for purpose) must be reviewed and approved by Legal before acceptance, and should not eliminate express warranties negotiated in the agreement.
2.4 Warranty remedies should include, at minimum, repair, replacement, or refund at the company's option.

3. APPLICABILITY
Applies to all agreements involving the purchase or licensing of goods, software, or services by the company.
