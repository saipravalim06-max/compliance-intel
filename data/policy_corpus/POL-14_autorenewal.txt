POLICY ID: POL-14
TITLE: Auto-Renewal and Contract Renewal Policy
CATEGORY: Renewal
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2024-01-01
OWNER: Procurement Department

1. PURPOSE
This policy governs the use of automatic renewal provisions in company contracts.

2. REQUIREMENTS
2.1 Any contract with an automatic renewal clause must provide the company with a non-renewal notice window of no less than sixty (60) calendar days prior to the renewal date.
2.2 Automatic renewal terms must not increase contract value by more than five percent (5%) per renewal period without prior Finance Department approval.
2.3 Contracts with automatic renewal clauses must include the renewal date and notice deadline in a clearly identifiable, non-buried section of the agreement.
2.4 Multi-year automatic renewal commitments (renewal terms exceeding twelve months) require Director-level approval before signature.

3. APPLICABILITY
Applies to all subscription, licensing, and service contracts containing automatic renewal provisions.
