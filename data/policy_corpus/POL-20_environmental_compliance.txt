POLICY ID: POL-20
TITLE: Environmental Compliance Policy
CATEGORY: Environmental
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2024-01-01
OWNER: Sustainability and Compliance Office

1. PURPOSE
This policy sets environmental compliance expectations for company operations and suppliers.

2. REQUIREMENTS
2.1 Suppliers must comply with all applicable environmental laws and hold valid permits for any activity with material environmental impact.
2.2 Manufacturing suppliers with annual contract value above USD 1,000,000 must hold ISO 14001 certification or an equivalent environmental management certification.
2.3 Any environmental incident or regulatory notice relating to the services provided to the company must be reported to the company within forty-eight (48) hours.
2.4 Environmental permits, monitoring results, and compliance records must be retained for a minimum of five (5) years.
2.5 Hazardous waste arising from work for the company must be handled and disposed of only by licensed handlers.

3. APPLICABILITY
Applies to company operations and to all suppliers and manufacturing partners.
