POLICY ID: POL-07
TITLE: Indemnification Policy
CATEGORY: Indemnification
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2024-01-01
OWNER: Legal Department

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines minimum indemnification protections required in company contracts.

2. REQUIREMENTS
2.1 Vendor and supplier contracts must include indemnification by the counterparty for third-party intellectual property infringement claims arising from the counterparty's products or services.
2.2 Indemnification obligations must cover reasonable attorneys' fees and costs of defense, not solely settlement or judgment amounts.
2.3 Data processing agreements must include indemnification for losses arising from the counterparty's failure to comply with applicable data protection law.
2.4 Indemnification obligations should not be subject to the general liability cap under POL-06 where they relate to IP infringement, data breaches, or confidentiality breaches.

3. APPLICABILITY
Applies to all contracts involving the provision of goods, services, software, or data processing by a third party to the company.
