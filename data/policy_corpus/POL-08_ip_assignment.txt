POLICY ID: POL-08
TITLE: Intellectual Property Assignment Policy
CATEGORY: Intellectual Property
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2024-01-01
OWNER: Legal Department

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines the company's standards for ownership of intellectual property created under contracted work.

2. REQUIREMENTS
2.1 Any work product created by a contractor or vendor specifically for the company under a paid engagement must be assigned to the company as a "work made for hire," with all rights, title, and interest vesting in the company upon creation or payment, whichever is earlier.
2.2 Background or pre-existing intellectual property of a vendor incorporated into deliverables must be licensed to the company on a perpetual, royalty-free, worldwide basis for the intended use.
2.3 Contracts must not grant the vendor ownership of any custom developments, data, or configurations created specifically for the company.
2.4 Any exceptions to full IP assignment require review and written approval by the Legal Department.

3. APPLICABILITY
Applies to all consulting, development, design, and custom software engagements entered into by the company.
