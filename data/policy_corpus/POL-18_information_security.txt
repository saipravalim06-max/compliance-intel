POLICY ID: POL-18
TITLE: Information Security Policy
CATEGORY: Information Security
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2024-01-01
OWNER: Information Security Office

1. PURPOSE
This policy sets minimum security safeguards for third parties that store, process, or have access to company or customer information.

2. REQUIREMENTS
2.1 Company and customer data must be encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard encryption (AES-256 or equivalent).
2.2 Multi-factor authentication is required for all administrative and remote access to systems holding company or customer data.
2.3 Third-party user access rights to company data must be reviewed at least once every ninety (90) days and revoked when no longer required.
2.4 Critical security vulnerabilities in systems handling company data must be remediated within fifteen (15) days of identification.
2.5 Vendors handling company data must undergo an independent security assessment or penetration test at least once every twelve (12) months and share a summary of results on request.
2.6 Any security incident affecting company or customer data must be reported to the Information Security Office within twenty-four (24) hours of discovery.

3. APPLICABILITY
Applies to all vendors, contractors, and service providers with access to company systems or information, and must be reflected in the applicable agreements.
