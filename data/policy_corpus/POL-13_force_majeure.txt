POLICY ID: POL-13
TITLE: Force Majeure Policy
CATEGORY: Force Majeure
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2024-01-01
OWNER: Legal Department

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines acceptable standards for force majeure provisions in company contracts.

2. REQUIREMENTS
2.1 Force majeure clauses must require the affected party to provide written notice within ten (10) business days of the event's onset, describing the nature and expected duration of the impact.
2.2 Force majeure provisions must not excuse payment obligations for services already rendered prior to the triggering event.
2.3 If a force majeure event continues for longer than sixty (60) consecutive days, either party should retain the right to terminate the agreement without penalty.
2.4 Pandemic, epidemic, and government-mandated shutdowns must be explicitly addressed as qualifying events where relevant to the contract's subject matter.

3. APPLICABILITY
Applies to all commercial contracts, particularly those involving ongoing service delivery or supply obligations.
