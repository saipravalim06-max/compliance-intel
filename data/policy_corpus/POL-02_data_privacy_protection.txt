POLICY ID: POL-02
TITLE: Data Privacy and Protection Policy
CATEGORY: Data Privacy
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2024-01-01
OWNER: Data Protection Office

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes minimum standards for the collection, processing, storage, and deletion of personal data handled by the company and its contracted third parties.

2. REQUIREMENTS
2.1 Any third party processing personal data on the company's behalf must delete or return all such data within thirty (30) days of contract termination, and provide written certification of deletion.
2.2 Cross-border transfer of personal data outside the country of collection requires an approved data transfer mechanism (e.g., standard contractual clauses) prior to transfer.
2.3 Any data breach involving company personal data must be reported to the Data Protection Office within twenty-four (24) hours of discovery by the processing party.
2.4 Contracts involving personal data processing must specify the categories of data processed, purpose of processing, and retention period.
2.5 Sub-processing of personal data by a vendor's own subcontractors requires prior written approval from the company.

3. APPLICABILITY
Applies to all contracts, vendors, and internal systems that collect, store, or process personal data of employees, customers, or other data subjects.
