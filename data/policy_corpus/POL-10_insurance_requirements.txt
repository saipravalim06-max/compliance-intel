POLICY ID: POL-10
TITLE: Insurance Requirements Policy
CATEGORY: Insurance
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2024-01-01
OWNER: Risk Management Department

1. PURPOSE
This policy sets minimum insurance coverage requirements for third parties contracting with the company.

2. REQUIREMENTS
2.1 Vendors providing services on company premises must maintain general liability insurance with a minimum coverage of USD 1,000,000 per occurrence.
2.2 Vendors processing company data must maintain cyber liability insurance with a minimum coverage of USD 2,000,000 per incident.
2.3 Professional services vendors must maintain professional indemnity (errors and omissions) insurance appropriate to the scope of engagement, with a minimum of USD 1,000,000 coverage.
2.4 Proof of insurance (certificate of insurance) must be provided prior to contract execution and renewed annually.

3. APPLICABILITY
Applies to all vendors, contractors, and service providers engaged by the company.
