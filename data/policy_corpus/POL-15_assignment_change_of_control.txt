POLICY ID: POL-15
TITLE: Assignment and Change of Control Policy
CATEGORY: Assignment
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2024-01-01
OWNER: Legal Department

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines the company's requirements regarding assignment of contracts and change of control provisions.

2. REQUIREMENTS
2.1 A counterparty must not assign or transfer a contract, in whole or in part, without the company's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.
2.2 A change of control of the counterparty (e.g., merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets) involving a direct competitor of the company entitles the company to terminate the agreement without penalty.
2.3 The company reserves the right to assign its own contracts to an affiliate or successor entity without requiring counterparty consent, provided the affiliate/successor assumes all obligations.
2.4 Notice of any change of control must be provided to the company within fifteen (15) business days of the event.

3. APPLICABILITY
Applies to all commercial contracts entered into by the company.
