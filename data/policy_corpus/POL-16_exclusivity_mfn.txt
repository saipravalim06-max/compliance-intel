POLICY ID: POL-16
TITLE: Exclusivity and Most-Favored-Nation Policy
CATEGORY: Exclusivity
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2024-01-01
OWNER: Legal Department

1. PURPOSE
This policy governs the use of exclusivity and most-favored-nation (MFN) provisions in company contracts.

2. REQUIREMENTS
2.1 Exclusivity commitments binding the company to a single vendor or partner must not exceed a duration of twenty-four (24) months without Executive Committee approval.
2.2 Most-favored-nation clauses requiring a vendor to offer the company terms no less favorable than those offered to other customers require Legal Department review prior to acceptance.
2.3 Exclusivity provisions must include defined performance thresholds that, if unmet by the counterparty, permit the company to terminate the exclusivity without penalty.
2.4 Category-wide exclusivity (restricting the company from engaging any competing vendor in an entire product category) is discouraged and requires General Counsel sign-off.

3. APPLICABILITY
Applies to vendor, supply, and strategic partnership agreements.
