POLICY ID: POL-09
TITLE: Non-Compete and Non-Solicitation Policy
CATEGORY: Non-Compete
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2024-01-01
OWNER: Legal & HR Department

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes acceptable limits for non-compete and non-solicitation provisions in company contracts and employment-adjacent agreements.

2. REQUIREMENTS
2.1 Non-compete provisions in vendor or partner agreements must not exceed a duration of twelve (12) months following termination of the agreement.
2.2 Non-compete provisions must be limited to a reasonably defined geographic scope and line of business directly related to the agreement's subject matter.
2.3 Non-solicitation of employee provisions may extend up to twenty-four (24) months following termination but must be mutual where possible.
2.4 Non-compete clauses that would restrict the company's ability to engage other providers in the same market segment are not acceptable without General Counsel approval.

3. APPLICABILITY
Applies to vendor, partnership, franchise, and consulting agreements that include restrictive covenants.
