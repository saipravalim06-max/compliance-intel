POLICY ID: POL-03
TITLE: Payment Terms Policy
CATEGORY: Payment
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2024-01-01
OWNER: Finance Department

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines standard payment terms for contracts entered into by the company with vendors, suppliers, and service providers.

2. REQUIREMENTS
2.1 Standard payment terms for vendor invoices shall be net forty-five (45) days from the date of a valid, undisputed invoice, unless otherwise approved by the Chief Financial Officer.
2.2 Late payment penalties or interest charges proposed by a counterparty in excess of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month require Finance Department approval prior to acceptance.
2.3 All payments in foreign currency must include a clearly defined exchange rate mechanism.
2.4 Advance payments or deposits exceeding twenty percent (20%) of total contract value require Director-level approval.
2.5 Contracts must not include automatic price escalation clauses exceeding five percent (5%) annually without Finance review.

3. APPLICABILITY
Applies to all contracts involving payment obligations by or to the company.
