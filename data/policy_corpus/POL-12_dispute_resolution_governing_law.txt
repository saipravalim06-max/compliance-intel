POLICY ID: POL-12
TITLE: Dispute Resolution and Governing Law Policy
CATEGORY: Dispute Resolution
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2024-01-01
OWNER: Legal Department

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes the company's preferred approach to dispute resolution and governing law provisions in contracts.

2. REQUIREMENTS
2.1 Contracts should specify a governing law consistent with the company's principal place of business, unless a different jurisdiction is required by the counterparty's regulatory environment and approved by Legal.
2.2 Mandatory binding arbitration clauses that waive the company's right to seek injunctive relief in court are not acceptable, except where injunctive relief remains expressly available as a carve-out.
2.3 Dispute resolution clauses should require a good-faith negotiation period of no less than thirty (30) days prior to initiation of formal proceedings.
2.4 Venue for any litigation or arbitration should be reasonably convenient and must not be unilaterally set solely at the counterparty's home jurisdiction without justification.

3. APPLICABILITY
Applies to all commercial contracts entered into by the company.
