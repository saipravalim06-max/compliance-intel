POLICY ID: POL-01
TITLE: Vendor Management Policy
CATEGORY: Vendor Management / Termination
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2024-01-01
OWNER: Procurement & Vendor Relations Department

1. PURPOSE
This policy governs the onboarding, management, and termination of third-party vendor relationships to ensure operational continuity and risk mitigation.

2. REQUIREMENTS
2.1 All vendor contracts must include a minimum thirty (30) calendar day written termination notice period for termination without cause, to allow sufficient time for service transition.
2.2 Vendor contracts involving access to company systems or data must undergo a security review prior to signature.
2.3 Vendors classified as "critical" (defined as vendors supporting core business operations) must provide a minimum sixty (60) day termination notice period.
2.4 All vendor agreements must designate a company-side relationship owner responsible for performance monitoring.
2.5 Vendor performance must be reviewed at least annually against agreed service levels.

3. APPLICABILITY
Applies to all procurement, IT, and business unit personnel entering into third-party vendor agreements on behalf of the company.
