1. Termination for Convenience
Either party may terminate this agreement at any time for convenience with 7 days written notice to the other party.

2. Commercial Payment Terms
The customer shall pay all undisputed vendor invoices within 90 days of invoice receipt.

3. Restraint of Trade and Non-Compete
The vendor covenants that during the term and for a period of 2 years following termination, it shall not engage in or provide competing services in the state.

4. Public Procurement and Turnover Criteria
The supplier warrants that it possesses an annual turnover of not less than 50 crore INR in each of the preceding three financial years.

5. Cybersecurity and Incident Notification
The vendor shall maintain cybersecurity safeguards and notify the client of any unauthorized data disclosure or breach within 30 days.

6. State Formalities and Stamp Duty
This commercial agreement shall be executed and performed without requiring separate state stamp duty or local registration.
