Kaveri Healthcare Private Limited - Procurement and Vendor Risk Policy
Policy No. KH-PRC-12 | Version 1.4 | Owner: Procurement and Legal
Purpose: This policy sets minimum terms for vendor agreements that involve patient data or clinical systems.
1. Commercial
1.1 Vendors must give the Company at least thirty (30) days written notice before terminating an agreement for convenience.
1.2 The Company must pay undisputed invoices within forty-five (45) days of receipt.
1.3 Vendor liability under any agreement must be no less than 12 months of fees paid.
1.4 Agreements must not renew automatically for a term longer than 12 months.
2. Security and Data
2.1 The vendor must maintain cyber liability insurance of at least INR 2 crore.
2.2 The vendor must report any security incident to the Company within 48 hours of becoming aware of it.
2.3 The vendor must not transfer patient data outside India without the prior written consent of the Company.
2.4 The vendor must delete all Company data within 60 days after termination.
2.5 The vendor must not engage sub-processors without the prior written approval of the Company.
3. Governance
3.1 The Company may audit vendor facilities and records on ten (10) business days notice.
3.2 The vendor must not use the Company's name or logo in marketing material without prior written approval.
3.3 The vendor must provide a warranty of at least 18 months.
3.4 The vendor must not assign or novate the agreement without the prior written consent of the Company.
3.5 The vendor must keep Company information confidential for at least 5 years after termination.
