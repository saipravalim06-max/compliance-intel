MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
This Master Services Agreement is entered into between Northwind Technologies Private Limited (the Company) and Helix Cloud Solutions LLP (the Supplier).
1.1 Services. Services means the cloud hosting, analytics and support services described in each Statement of Work issued under this Agreement.
1.2 Term. This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 36 months.
2.1 Payment. The Company must pay valid supplier invoices within 20 days of receipt.
2.2 Late Fees. Late payments will accrue interest at two percent per month until paid in full.
3.1 Renewal. This Agreement renews automatically for successive 12 month terms unless either party gives 15 days written notice of non-renewal.
3.2 Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause by giving 15 days written notice.
4.1 Limitation of Liability. The Supplier liability cap must be 3 months of fees paid under the agreement.
4.2 Insurance. The Supplier must maintain professional indemnity insurance of INR 1 crore throughout the term.
4.3 Warranty. The Supplier must provide a warranty period of 24 months from delivery.
5.1 Data Sharing. The Supplier may disclose customer data to third parties, including analytics partners, without prior written approval from the Company.
5.2 Breach Notification. The Supplier must notify the Company of any personal data breach within 7 days of discovery.
5.3 Data Deletion. The Supplier must delete all Company data within 5 years after termination of the agreement.
5.4 Confidentiality. The Supplier must keep Company information confidential for at least 5 years after termination.
6.1 Audit. The Company may audit supplier records once per year on 30 days notice.
6.2 Assignment. The Supplier may assign this agreement to any affiliate or successor without the prior written consent of the Company.
6.3 Governing Law. This agreement must be governed by the laws of India.
6.4 Subcontracting. The Supplier may subcontract core services to third parties without prior written approval from the Company.
6.5 Notices. Notices under this Agreement must be sent by email to the addresses stated in the Order Form.
