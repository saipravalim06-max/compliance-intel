SOFTWARE LICENCE AND SUPPORT AGREEMENT
This Agreement is made between Kaveri Healthcare Private Limited (the Company) and Orbit Analytics LLP (Orbit).
1.1 Definitions. Software means the clinical analytics platform and any updates that Orbit makes available to the Company.
1.2 Term. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and runs for an initial term of thirty-six (36) months.
2.1 Payment. The Company shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) business days of receipt.
2.2 Late Fees. Overdue amounts will accrue interest at two percent per month until paid in full.
3.1 Termination. Orbit may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving the Company not less than four weeks written notice.
3.2 Renewal. Unless a party gives notice of non-renewal, this Agreement renews automatically for successive terms of twenty-four (24) months.
4.1 Liability. Orbit's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed six (6) months of the fees paid.
4.2 Insurance. Orbit shall maintain cyber liability insurance cover. The minimum limit of that cover shall be INR 150 lakh.
4.3 Warranty. Orbit warrants that the Software will conform to its documentation for twenty-four (24) months from delivery.
5.1 Incident Reporting. Orbit shall notify the Company of any security incident without undue delay and in any event within two (2) days of discovery.
5.2 Data Location. Orbit may host patient data in data centres located in Singapore, provided that the Company has approved that location in writing beforehand.
5.3 Data Return. After termination Orbit shall delete all Company data within ninety (90) days, except where retention is required by applicable law.
5.4 Sub-processors. Orbit shall not appoint any sub-processor unless the Company has given its prior written approval.
5.5 Confidentiality. Orbit's confidentiality obligations shall continue for three (3) years after termination.
6.1 Audit. The Company may audit Orbit's records on fifteen (15) business days notice.
6.2 Publicity. Orbit shall not use the Company's name or logo in any publicity without the Company's prior written approval.
6.3 Transfer. Either party may novate this Agreement to a group company without notice to the other party.
6.4 Support. Orbit shall provide telephone support from 9 am to 6 pm, Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays.
6.5 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of India and the courts at Bengaluru have exclusive jurisdiction.
6.6 Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for any delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control.
